About me

Hi, I’m Toby. I’m a product designer with a background in software engineering and more than five years of experience working across startups, SaaS, and Web3 products.

My work usually lives where product thinking, interface craft, and implementation reality meet. I like turning messy ideas into systems and flows that feel clear to users and practical for teams to build.

Design philosophy

Most of the problems I’m drawn to involve some kind of complexity: operational workflows, financial behavior, emerging technology, or products still trying to find their shape.

My approach is to reduce friction without flattening the nuance. Good design should guide decisions, create trust, and make complicated systems feel intuitive enough that people can move with confidence.
