About Toby.

I'm a product designer with a builder's mindset and a bachelor's degree in software engineering. Over the last 5 years, I've worked across startups, enterprise products and Web3 startups, turning complex challenges into simple, meaningful designs through user research.

Because I understand how products get built, I don't just design for aesthetics. I design for flow, feasibility, and adoption. The goal is always the same: make the product more intuitive, and easier to trust.
